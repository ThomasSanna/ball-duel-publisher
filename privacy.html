Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Ball Duel Publisher runs only on its owner's computer and has no other users.

  • Data accessed: the TikTok access and refresh tokens of the owner's account, and the basic profile information returned with them.
  • Storage: tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.
  • Use: tokens are used only to upload the owner's videos as drafts and to check upload status.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • Deletion: access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings, and the local file can be deleted.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
